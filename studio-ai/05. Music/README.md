# Music library

Music beds for AI studio clips (the **Music** menu in the AI studio panel). Drop MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, AIFF or FLAC files
here, or use **Upload music…** in the panel. The music plays under the clip, dips automatically while someone speaks, and
fades in and out.

Only add music your brand is licensed to use in client videos. The files stay on this Mac (they aren't in git or in
the team package).
