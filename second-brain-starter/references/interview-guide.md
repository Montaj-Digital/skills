# Interview guide

Use this when starting from scratch. Ask one main question at a time. Don't turn this into a survey dump unless the person explicitly asks to answer in bulk.

## Opening

Start with:

```text
I'll help you build the first useful version of your AI second brain. We'll keep it small: one scope, one source, one reusable workflow and one review habit. I'll ask one question at a time and save the useful context into files you can reuse.
```

## Question path

### 1. Scope

Ask:

```text
What should this first brain be for: your role, your business, a project, a client, a content system or something else?
```

Capture:

- Brain name.
- Audience.
- Who can edit or approve it.
- What should stay private.

### 2. Outcome

Ask:

```text
What should AI be able to help with once this brain exists?
```

Capture:

- The first useful task.
- What a good answer or output looks like.
- What AI usually gets wrong without context.

### 3. Stable context

Ask:

```text
What facts should AI know before helping you here?
```

Follow up only where useful:

- Who you are.
- What the business or project does.
- Audience or customer.
- Offer, product, service or deliverable.
- Tone, standards or constraints.
- Things AI must not promise.

### 4. Current priorities

Ask:

```text
What matters most right now, and what is unresolved?
```

Capture:

- Active goals.
- Decisions already made.
- Open questions.
- Risks or sensitivities.

### 5. Sources

Ask:

```text
What source should we start with: notes, a transcript, a proposal, a brief, a website, a document or something pasted here?
```

If the person has no file ready, ask them to paste rough notes. A rough source is enough for a first version.

### 6. Systems and connections

Ask:

```text
Where does the live information for this area currently live?
```

Prompt categories:

- Calendar.
- Email.
- Meetings and transcripts.
- Tasks.
- Documents.
- CRM or customer data.
- Finance.
- Knowledge base.
- Chat or community.

Label each as:

- Manual import.
- Verified access.
- Proposed connection.

### 7. Review habit

Ask:

```text
After what kind of work should this brain be updated?
```

Examples:

- After client calls.
- After project meetings.
- After a proposal changes.
- After a decision is approved.
- Weekly review.

## Closing

End the interview with:

```text
I have enough to build the first version. I'll create the source-backed files, mark the gaps, then test whether a fresh AI answer can use the saved context without making things up.
```
